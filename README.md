## WORK FLOW ##

1. you need a sprite png just like this:
<img width="929" height="896" alt="beastfolk" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c72f7e7c-a3b8-44ef-81e6-3ac01f1e4f60" />

2. now you need a magenta background and put you sprite into that magenta background . make sure to use magenta background , it is important
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="Solid_magenta svg" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb56f9ba-35ac-4767-8cc1-3d186a40f78b" />

3. then put you sprite into that bg using any tool (im using excalidraw )
<img width="1913" height="962" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e8b20e07-0571-4b11-8ac0-086af3c54898" />

4. then take the screenshot with snipping tool just like this
<img width="1335" height="752" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b44563ea-1e34-44c1-8d18-8f6f56339de6" />

5. go to google flow and tell the agent to give the sprite Subtle movement or any kind of move you want , also tell the agent to maintain character consistency .
I'm using Omni 1.1 flash model to generate the video will take your flow 15 credits(10 sec video).
<img width="1918" height="902" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9749126c-d26a-40a4-acb2-21b4d85e432d" />
then it will generate you a video

<img width="1137" height="351" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b564fef6-c4ca-4eb8-ba44-6a71654d1ad6" />
[Watch the video that flow generated](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b410a61b-e883-473a-b3d9-e05308056859)


6. use this open source software with this [link](https://strong-basbousa-125955.netlify.app/) or run it on your system.
  1. upload your video here
  <img width="1918" height="885" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/775555b5-b420-484e-a791-33215f94de76" />
  2. you now can select how many frames you want, trim the video and all. then you hit extract frames button.
  <img width="880" height="712" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/61175ab6-14c9-4120-ae85-b2ca161af940" />
  3. after that , scroll down and you will find preview of your animation frame by frame , if you like it you can export it with your preferable resoluiton
  <img width="1907" height="853" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/80218798-81dd-43ed-8e32-bd36e62eaf04" />
  <img width="561" height="552" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c2c23fe2-24d6-410a-8a2c-446fde839b27" />
  4. after that you can download as a zip or as a sprite sheet png. extract that zip file and you will find your frames
  <img width="1667" height="598" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/631ee4fd-cde4-4a2d-8cc5-4c2743b12656" />
  5. then go to Magenta remover section and upload all you sprite frames
  <img width="1918" height="903" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e3a03a22-b4bd-4f58-aa7b-d804fbdffc20" />
  6. it will automatically remove the magenta bg
